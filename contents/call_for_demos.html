EDBT 2027 Demo Track Call for Papers

The annual International Conference on Extending Database Technology (EDBT) is a leading international forum for researchers, developers, and users to present and discuss cutting-edge ideas, and to exchange techniques, tools and experiences related to data management.

The EDBT demonstration track has become an important venue for sharing prototype systems with the broader data management community. We solicit original demonstration proposals that will showcase the most exciting new data management technologies.

Important dates

All deadlines are at 5 pm PT (Pacific Time)

  • November 18, 2026: Paper submission
  • January 20, 2027: Notification of accept/reject
  • February 1, 2027: Camera-Ready copy due

Topics of interest

We welcome submissions related to all aspects of data management, particularly topics as specified in the EDBT call for research papers and topics of emerging interest in the database research and development community. Demonstrations may consist of software, hardware, or both. The emphasis of the EDBT demonstration program is on visionary, next-generation systems requiring significant research and development effort.

Evaluation criteria

Selection for presentation at EDBT is highly competitive, with the ideal demonstration showcasing recent research achievements and combining novelty with an exciting audience experience. The proposal should clearly identify new intellectual contributions of the system and its architecture. It must include citations of the most relevant papers describing the research and innovations it presents and any previous publications of the system itself, including versions with significant overlap in features and functionality.

The proposal should also describe in detail what EDBT attendees will experience. What exactly will the audience see? Will they be able to interact with the system? Is there an interesting scenario or script that the demonstrators will use? Are there any requirements beyond a desk and typical Wi-Fi Internet access? How will these requirements be met at the conference and how would audience experience be affected if they are not met?

Finally, it is mandatory for authors to include a short video (up to 5 minutes long) of their demonstration in MP4 format, if possible using the H.264 video codec and AAC audio codec, to illustrate the user experience and the demo scenario.

Conflicts of interest (COI)

The EDBT 2027 demo track follows the same COI policies as the research track as explained in the EDBT call for research papers.

Duplicate submissions

A demo paper submitted to EDBT 2027 must not be under review for any other publishing forum or presentation venue, including conferences, workshops, and journals, during the time it is being considered for EDBT. Furthermore, after authors submit their work to EDBT, they must await the response from EDBT and only resubmit elsewhere if their paper is rejected (or withdrawn at their request) from EDBT.

Submission guidelines

All submissions will be electronic via CMT at https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/EDBT2027.

 

Length: Demo papers (including bibliographic references) must not exceed 4 pages.

Submission system will open about two weeks before the submission deadline.

File type: Each paper is to be submitted as a single PDF file. Submitted papers must print without difficulty on a variety of printers, using Adobe Acrobat Reader. It is the responsibility of the authors to ensure that their submitted PDF file will print easily on simple default configurations.

Papers should be formatted according to the latest ACM Proceedings Format without any change in terms of fonts, margins, inter-column spacing, style, footers, etc. EDBT uses non-anonymous submissions, which means that the authors must list their names and affiliations as part of their submission. Reviewers, on the other hand, are anonymous to the authors. Any submitted paper violating the length, file type, or formatting requirements will be desk rejected. Detailed instructions and templates can be found in the EDBT call for research papers.

Procedure for withdrawal of submissions

Authors may withdraw their submissions within one week following the submission deadline. To request a withdrawal, authors must email the PC chairs, copying all co-authors in the message and explicitly stating that all authors consent to the withdrawal.
