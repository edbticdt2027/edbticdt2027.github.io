EDBT 2027 - Young Researcher Symposium


In alignment with the YRS 2026, the young researcher symposium at EDBT 2027 aims to shift from a conventional paper-presentation format toward an engaging, interactive, and community-driven experience. This format is designed to foster active participation, peer-to-peer connections, and mentorship opportunities that support young researchers’ long-term academic and professional growth.

Instead of focusing solely on research talks, the workshop will include a diverse set of activities that encourage meaningful engagement and knowledge exchange. These include invited talks that offer insights into emerging research directions and professional development; structured networking sessions that provide opportunities for students to interact with peers, mentors, and senior researchers; and a personalized mentoring program that connects students with mentors based on their research interests and goals.

Through these activities, the workshop aims to cultivate a supportive and inclusive environment where PhD students can exchange ideas, receive constructive feedback, explore collaborations, and gain guidance on both research and career development. Ultimately, the redesigned EDBT 2027 PhD Workshop seeks to strengthen the sense of community within the data management field and empower the next generation of researchers to engage confidently and creatively with the challenges ahead.
