EDBT 2027 Industrial and Application Track Call for Papers

The annual International Conference on Extending Database Technology (EDBT) is a premier international forum for researchers, developers, and users to present and discuss cutting-edge ideas, and to exchange techniques, tools and experiences related to database technology and data management. The track offers a platform to discuss the latest advances, trends, and challenges in data systems, from applied research to industrial applications.

Industrial & Application

EDBT invites industrial and application papers that describe important industrial advances and application achievements in all in areas including, but not limited to:

  • database and data management systems
  • scalable machine learning and data mining systems
  • stream data processing
  • information retrieval
  • knowledge discovery
  • data quality, cleaning and preparation
  • information integration
  • cloud-based systems
  • mobile data management
  • data systems on new hardware
  • multi-model database systems
  • testing and benchmarking data management solutions
  • security aspects of data processing systems
  • Large Language Models (LLMs) in data management and analytics
  • AI-powered systems for relational and tabular data
  • synergies between machine learning, AI, and data management
  • data management for AI agents and agentic applications, including memory, state, retrieval, and orchestration

It therefore encourages submissions related to all aspects of data management and data engineering as well as work on applying recent research advances to real-world problems and industrial systems, particularly with a focus on the synergies between data management and machine learning. Moreover, new applications, novel systems, challenging real-world use cases, practical customer case-studies, insights on the legal, social, and ethical implications of database technology, as well as analysis of applied technology in an industrial context are highly welcome. Submissions that do not articulate the innovative aspect of a product or application, but rather give a general overview, will not be accepted.

Each submission must include at least one author affiliated with a non-academic affiliation during which the project was undertaken.

Industrial and application papers are not required to introduce a new algorithm or conventional research contribution. Contributions may instead arise from novel system architectures, engineering solutions, applications of existing techniques to challenging real-world problems, production deployments, operational experience, large-scale empirical studies, or insights that are difficult to obtain outside real-world environments. Submissions should clearly articulate the technical contribution and the insights that generalize beyond the specific product, organization, or application being described.

Authorship and Conflicts of interest (COI)

All authors must be registered on the CMT site and provide an ORCID. All authors take full responsibility for the content of the paper. The list of authors in CMT must be identical to the list of authors noted on the pdf file.

During the submission of a paper, the submission site will request information about conflicts of interest of the paper's authors with program committee (PC) members. It is the full responsibility of all authors of a paper to identify all and only the PC members with whom they have a conflict of interest (COI), as defined below.

  • The PC member is a co-author of the paper.
  • The PC member has been a co-worker in the same organization within the past 5 years or will be doing so in the next 6 months on account of an accepted job offer.
  • The PC member has been a collaborator or coauthor within the past 5 years.
  • The PC member is or was the author's primary thesis advisor, no matter how long ago.
  • The author is or was the PC member's primary thesis advisor, no matter how long ago.
  • The PC member is a relative or close personal friend of the author.

Papers with incorrect or incomplete authorship and conflict of interest information as of the submission closing time are subject to immediate rejection. To identify any potentially spurious conflicts, PC members will be given a list of submitting authors who have indicated that they have a conflict with that PC member.

Duplicate Submissions and Novelty Requirements

A paper submitted to EDBT cannot be under review at any other publishing forum or presentation venue - including conferences, workshops, and journals - during the entire period when the paper is being considered for EDBT. Furthermore, after a paper is submitted to EDBT, the authors must await the response and only then resubmit elsewhere if the paper is rejected (or withdrawn at their request) from EDBT.

All papers submitted to EDBT must present substantial new contributions not described in any prior publication. A prior publication is either (a) a paper of five pages or more presented, or accepted for presentation, at a refereed conference or workshop with proceedings; or (b) an article published, or accepted for publication, in a refereed journal. If an EDBT submission overlaps with a prior publication, the submission must cite the prior publication and clearly explain the relationship between the two. Contributions may arise from novel system designs, engineering techniques, deployment experience, application of existing techniques in challenging environments, production-scale evaluation, operational lessons, or insights from real-world use—and that a new algorithm or traditional research contribution is not required.

Any violation of the duplicate submission and novelty requirements will result in an immediate rejection of the submitted paper.

Formatting and Submission Guidelines

All submissions will be electronic via CMT at .

All authors are required to register themselves into CMT. We expect authors to have a single unique ORCID, they are required to declare at the time of submission. We also encourage authors to inform their DBLP ID. This can be done according to the guidelines here: https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/docs/help/general/external-profile.html. Both IDs will be used to check for COIs.

Conflicts of interest can be declared on CMT by clicking on your name (upper-right-hand-side on CMT) and selecting "Domain Conflict" and "Individual Conflicts". An author's declared conflicts will be automatically applied to all of their submissions (conflicts are not declared per submission).

Papers should be formatted according to the following LaTeX template without any change in terms of fonts, margins, inter-column spacing, style, footers, and so on: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dtNEVPLPNTlKm1_agOxlHWuQK-Szd8Lo/view?usp=drive_link

Overleaf authors can copy the following project: https://de.overleaf.com/read/khczkkygdrtt#aeaa28.

Papers must be at most 8 pages long, with additional unlimited pages for references only. All content, including any appendices but excluding the references, must fit on the given number of pages.

Each paper is to be submitted as a single PDF file. Submitted papers must print without difficulty on a variety of printers using Adobe Acrobat Reader. It is the responsibility of the authors to ensure that their submitted PDF file prints easily on simple default configurations.

EDBT employs single-anonymous reviewing, which requires authors to include their names and affiliations as part of their submission. Reviewers, on the other hand, are anonymous to the authors.

Any submitted paper violating the length, file type, or formatting requirements will be desk-rejected. Once the paper is submitted, EDBT does not accept any changes to the title and/or authorship list after a paper has been submitted.

Artifact Availability

Authors must submit supplemental material, when feasible, such as code, data, and other implementation artifacts used to produce the results reported in the paper. Inability to release proprietary artifacts will not itself count against the submission if the paper adequately explains the restriction and provides enough methodological detail to assess the work. Reviewers will have access to the supplemental material and consider it in their evaluation of the technical quality and credibility of the contribution. If the authors cannot submit supplemental material, this must be documented by the authors during the submission process (e.g., industrial research, etc.).

Authors can submit (a) a single zip file as supplemental material or (b) include a link to the supplemental material (e.g., a link to a GitHub repository). Links are only allowed when there is no access-monitoring to the webpage, to preserve the anonymity of the reviewers.

The text of the paper must have a section named Artifacts right before the references, which provides an explanation on access and use of all the artifacts for that paper. If no artifacts are available, the author should provide an explanation in that section. This section does not count towards the submission page limit.
